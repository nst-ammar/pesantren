## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the dignity, spiritual tranquility, and academic excellence of a modern Islamic boarding school (Pesantren). It synthesizes traditional Islamic heritage with contemporary institutional clarity. 

The aesthetic blends **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Tonal Layering**. Generous negative space, disciplined hierarchy, and deep chromatic values project prestige and moral integrity. The experience must feel serene, trustworthy, disciplined, and forward-looking—never noisy or ornate for the sake of decoration. Traditional Islamic geometry is interpreted through minimal, fine-line SVG patterns and watermarked backdrops rather than loud ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette establishes moral gravitas and spiritual vitality through graded emeralds and energetic accents.

- **Primary (`#004b23`)**: Deep forest green anchoring typography, key navigational containers, and primary call-to-actions. Represents stability, tradition, and intellectual gravitas.
- **Secondary (`#38b000`)**: Vibrant emerald accent for interactive focus points, status chips, dynamic metrics, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#007200`)**: Bridging mid-tone green used for secondary surfaces, subheaders, and framing borders.
- **Neutral (`#f7f9f6`)**: Warm alabaster-ivory canvas providing breathing room, warmth, and legibility, avoiding cold digital whites.

### Named Color Accents & Backgrounds
- `emerald-deep` (`#006400`) and `forest-rich` (`#008000`): Used for tonal card backgrounds and gradient overlays when coupled with the primary base.
- `lime-vibrant` (`#70e000`), `lime-glow` (`#9ef01a`), and `lime-tint` (`#ccff33`): Applied sparingly for micro-badges, achievement metrics, and ambient light sources against dark green sections.
- `charcoal-ink` (`#142118`): High-legibility deep evergreen-charcoal for long-form body copy.
- `gold-accent` (`#c59b27`): Reserved for honorary badges, quranic citations, and graduation achievements.

## Typography

Typography pairs classical authority with modern warmth and high legibility:

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles**: **Playfair Display** introduces scholarly cadence, classical stature, and prestige. It represents wisdom, tradition, and academic gravity. It must never be set in all caps.
- **Body & Functional Text**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers a clean, open, approachable reading rhythm with contemporary geometric balance. Its high x-height maintains effortless legibility across program descriptions, administrative schedules, and parent portals.
- **Arabic / Script Quotations**: When presenting Arabic scripture or hadith references, wrap them in clean italicized serif styling accompanied by distinct line-height padding (`1.8`) to honor calligraphic space.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured upon a **12-column responsive grid** bounded within a maximum content container of `1280px`. 

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `4rem` outer boundary margins. Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` to `6rem`) fosters a serene, unhurried browsing cadence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margins. Content groups (such as academic tracks and boarding life features) shift into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` margins. Complex institutional data reflows vertically into single-column cards with persistent action bars.
- **Islamic Geometry Framing**: Subtle decorative grid borders (0.5px opacity) align cards to golden-ratio inspired structural intervals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal surface stacking** rather than generic grey dropshadows.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Neutral ivory (`#f7f9f6`) ground.
- **Level 1 (Card/Container Surfaces)**: Pure white (`#ffffff`) surfaces featuring a crisp low-contrast border: `1px solid rgba(0, 75, 35, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements)**: Tinted ambient shadow with soft emerald dispersion: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(0, 75, 35, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Hero Overlays)**: Deep ambient immersion: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 43, 20, 0.25)`.
- **Inverted Deep Green Containers**: For the hero showcase, prayer timetables, and admissions callouts, surfaces use `#004b23` with inner borders of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` and lime-tinted backglows (`rgba(56, 176, 0, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Roundedness Level 2** (`rounded: 0.5rem`, `rounded-lg: 1rem`, `rounded-xl: 1.5rem`).

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, notification badges) employ `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii to remain disciplined, scholarly, and modern.
- Visual feature containers, dormitory gallery viewports, and primary cards use `1rem` (`16px`) radii for approachable architecture.
- Full pills are strictly reserved for status indicators, active tabs, and category filters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid deep forest green (`#004b23`) with white text (`#ffffff`), `0.5rem` radius, padded `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover transitions smoothly into `#006400` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(0, 75, 35, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary**: Clear ivory background with a 1.5px solid border in `#004b23`, text in `#004b23`. On hover, fills with `#eef2eb`.
- **Vibrant Accent**: Secondary green (`#38b000`) fill with `#ffffff` text, applied exclusively for the primary CTA (e.g., "Daftar Sekarang / Apply Online").

### Cards & Modules
- Structured on pure white backgrounds (`#ffffff`), bounded by a subtle border (`rgba(0, 75, 35, 0.08)`) with `1rem` corners.
- Featured program cards (e.g., Tahfidz Al-Qur'an, International Cambridge Track) feature an elevated header chip and an optional fine-line arched top border referencing classic Islamic arches.

### Chips & Badges
- Metric chips and status badges utilize translucent tint fills: `rgba(56, 176, 0, 0.1)` background with `#006400` bold text.
- Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padded `0.25rem 0.75rem`.

### Input Fields & Form Controls
- Inputs use `#ffffff` background with `1px solid rgba(0, 75, 35, 0.2)`, `0.5rem` border radius, and label font weights of 600.
- Focused state transitions to a 2px outline in `#38b000` with an outer ring in `rgba(56, 176, 0, 0.15)`.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons use `#004b23` active states with pure white iconography.

### Prayer & Academic Schedule Table
- Clean alternating rows between ivory (`#f7f9f6`) and white (`#ffffff`).
- Active period highlighted with a left border accent in `#38b000` (3px solid) and soft lime background tint (`rgba(112, 224, 0, 0.06)`).
